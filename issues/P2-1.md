# P2-1 — A transparent PNG loses its alpha channel on a plain load-then-save, …

## Finding / 问题

- EN: A transparent PNG loses its alpha channel on a plain load-then-save, and under both watermarks, because `imagecreatefrompng()` leaves `imagesavealpha` off. Fixed in the working tree by setting it for PNG and WEBP in `createFromFile()`, with four tests.

- Where / 位置: `src/GDImage.php:311-313 · tests/GDImageTest.php:139-202`
- Verification / 验证: reproduced / 已实证
